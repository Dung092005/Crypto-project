Raw OpenAlex/Crossref records and Crossref BibTeX exports are in `01-raw/`; exact queries are in `01-search-log.md`. DOI metadata was compared with publisher/standards pages. `INACCESSIBLE` means the paywalled full text was not read; only the accessible publisher abstract/metadata may support claims.

**Claim/source alignment.** RSA arithmetic derives from `rivest1978method`; the project's exact subset threshold follows directly from CRT plus its own derivation and raw E1 data, while `hastad1988solving` supplies broader historical context. OAEP encoding details come from `moriarty2016pkcs1`, with origin attributed to `bellare1995optimal`. GCM/AAD terminology comes from `dworkin2007gcm`. All numeric BroadcastGuard outcomes come from the official local dataset, never from these papers.

**Important nuance:** RFC 8017 §7.1 discusses security in the random-oracle setting and notes limits of the reduction for concrete parameters. The report should describe the tested CRT-and-root failure as an observation; it need not make a formal CCA2 claim.
