## Prior-work scope

The manuscript's five references resolve from `research/broadcastguard/REFERENCES.bib`. [Rivest–Shamir–Adleman 1978](https://doi.org/10.1145/359340.359342) grounds RSA's public-key arithmetic; [Håstad 1988](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0217019) supplies broader low-degree modular context, while the fixed-message inequality is derived locally. [Bellare–Rogaway 1995](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/BFb0053428) grounds probabilistic OAEP encoding; RFC 8017 defines the implemented scheme; NIST SP 800-38D supports GCM/AAD terminology. The Håstad and Bellare–Rogaway full texts were not obtained; no detailed theorem is quoted from them.
